Frequently asked questions

Answers for customers, retailers and buyers. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

For Customers

Start on our Sell Your Device page: select your device, add its details and share your contact information. A partnered retailer verifies the device, buyers make offers, and you choose the one you want. You can also walk into any TEKRIO partnered retailer.
Offers are made by independent, verified buyers based on your device's model and inspected condition. You see every offer before you decide — not just one shop's quote — and you're under no obligation to accept.
Payment is completed at the partnered retailer once you accept an offer and hand over your device. We recommend backing up and resetting your device before the sale.
TEKRIO is built to be transparent — the offer you see is the offer you accept. If anything about the device differs from the inspection, the retailer will explain it before you confirm.

For Retailers

Fill out the registration form on our For Retailers page with your store name, owner details, mobile, city and GSTIN (optional). Our onboarding team will contact you next.

You earn a commission on every device transacted through TEKRIO at your store. Commission details are shared during onboarding.
