How TEKRIO works. Simple. Transparent. Your choice.

From selecting your device to completing the sale, here's exactly what happens on the TEKRIO marketplace — and what the retailer and buyers do behind the scenes.

The five steps

1

Select device

Choose whether it's an Apple or Android device and enter the model — online through Sell Your Device, or at a partnered retailer.

2

Add details

Share storage, RAM, condition and accessories. A partnered retailer then inspects the device and records photos and IMEI.

3

Get offers

The inspected device is listed on the TEKRIO marketplace feed, where verified buyers make their offers.

4

Choose offer

See every buyer offer side by side and choose the one you want. You're under no obligation to accept.

5

Complete transaction

Confirm the sale at the retailer, hand over your device and get paid.

Customer  |  Retailer  |  Buyer

What each side sees

Customer

A list of buyer offers for their exact device and condition, in rupees, with no obligation until they choose to accept one.

Retailer

A guided inspection and listing flow in the TEKRIO app — add device details, condition and photos, and let buyers make offers instead of chasing buyers themselves.

Buyer

A marketplace feed of inspected, IMEI-recorded devices to make offers on, so sourcing genuine stock takes less time and fewer field visits.

Transparency, with no shortcuts

Every offer is visible before you decide

Buyers are verified before they can make offers, and every device is inspected by a partnered retailer before it is listed. There's no back-and-forth haggling — you see the offers on your device and choose the one that suits you.
