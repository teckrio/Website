How TEKRIO works? Simple. Transparent. Best price.

From walking into a store to getting paid, here's exactly what happens to your device on TEKRIO — and what happens behind the scenes for retailers and vendors.

1

Visit retailer

Bring your old phone to your nearest TEKRIO partnered retailer. No appointment needed — just walk in like you would for any exchange.

2

Get best offers

The retailer inspects the device and lists it on TEKRIO with its grade, photos and IMEI. Verified vendors across the network compete in real time to give the best price.

3

Compare & close

You see every verified offer side by side on the retailer's screen, pick the best one, and get paid instantly at the store — with your data securely wiped.

Behind the scenes

What each side sees

Customer

A live list of verified offers for their exact device and condition, in rupees, with no obligation until they choose to accept one.

Retailer

A 30-second listing flow in the TEKRIO app — add device details, grade and photos, and let vendors bid instead of chasing buyers themselves.

Vendor

A pan-India feed of graded, IMEI-verified devices to bid on, so sourcing genuine stock takes minutes, not field visits.
