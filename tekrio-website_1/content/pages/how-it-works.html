How TEKRIO works. Simple. Transparent. Your choice.

From selecting your device to completing the sale, here's exactly what happens on the TEKRIO marketplace — and what the retailer and buyers do behind the scenes.

Step by step

Five steps from your drawer to paid

Scroll to follow one device through the TEKRIO marketplace.

  1. Step 1

    Select device

    Choose whether it's an Apple or Android device and enter the model — online through Sell Your Device, or at a partnered store.

  2. Step 2

    Add details

    Share storage, RAM, condition and accessories. Online, you add photos of each side; at a store, the store inspects the device and records photos and IMEI.

  3. Step 3

    Get offers

    Your device goes live on the TEKRIO marketplace, where verified buyers compete with their bids for a few minutes.

  4. Step 4

    Choose offer

    You get the best offer — by email if you sold online. Accept it, or let it go. You're under no obligation to accept.

  5. Step 5

    Complete transaction

    Online: take the phone to the buyer's shop, where they check it and pay you directly. At a store: hand it over and get paid there.

Two ways to sell

Online from home, or at a partnered store

Sell online

  1. Describe your device and add photos on Sell Your Device.
  2. Verified buyers bid; we email you the best offer.
  3. Accept it within a few hours to see the buyer's shop address and phone.
  4. Visit the shop within 48 hours with the phone and a photo ID. The buyer checks it and pays you directly, by UPI or cash.
  5. Confirm the payment on your offer page and give the buyer the code it shows — that completes the sale.

Sell at a partnered store

  1. Walk into a TEKRIO partnered store with your device.
  2. The store inspects it and lists it with photos and IMEI.
  3. Verified buyers bid while you wait.
  4. Accept the best offer, or walk away.
  5. Complete the sale at the store and get paid there.

What each side sees

Customer

The best buyer offer for their exact device and condition, in rupees, with no obligation until they accept it — and the buyer's shop details once they do.

Retailer

A guided inspection and listing flow in the TEKRIO app — add device details, condition and photos, and let buyers make offers instead of chasing buyers themselves.

Buyer

A marketplace feed of store-inspected and seller-listed devices with photos to bid on, so sourcing genuine stock takes less time and fewer field visits.

Transparency, with no shortcuts

You see the offer before you decide

Buyers are verified before they can bid, and the highest bid becomes your offer. There's no back-and-forth haggling: if a buyer finds the condition differs from what was described, they can propose a lower price, and it only changes if you approve it.
