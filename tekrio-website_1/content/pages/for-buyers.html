How sourcing works on TEKRIO

From listing to inventory, without the guesswork

Partnered stores inspect and list devices with condition details, photos and IMEI. You see them in the marketplace feed of the TEKRIO Buyer app and make your offer — when the customer chooses your offer, the device is yours.

  2. Verification — our team checks your GST or PAN and business details.

  4. Start buying — sign in and make offers from your marketplace feed.
