Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions

Operated by Anabriya Technologies LLP for the TEKRIO platform (www.tekrio.in). Last updated: 19 September 2026.

This page is a plain-language starting point prepared alongside the website build. Anabriya Technologies LLP should have it reviewed by legal counsel before go-live, particularly the data-protection sections, to ensure full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any other applicable law.

Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

  • Customers: name, mobile number, city, and phone brand/model submitted when checking a price or visiting a partnered retailer.
  • Retailers: store name, owner name, mobile number, city, and GSTIN (optional) submitted during registration.
  • Vendors: business name, mobile number, city, and GST/PAN submitted during registration.
  • Automatically: basic usage data (pages visited, device/browser type) via Google Analytics, once enabled, to help us improve the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To connect customers with a nearby TEKRIO partnered retailer.
  • To verify and onboard retailers and vendors onto the TEKRIO network.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact and partnership forms.
  • To improve the website and communicate service updates.

3. How we share your information

We share customer device details with the specific retailer and competing verified vendors involved in that exchange only. We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may share information where required by law.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including SSL encryption in transit, to protect the information you submit. Device data wiped as part of an exchange is handled at the retailer location as part of the exchange process.

5. Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@tekrio.in.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use www.tekrio.in. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Terms & Conditions

1. About TEKRIO

TEKRIO (www.tekrio.in) is a platform operated by Anabriya Technologies LLP that connects customers, retailers and vendors to facilitate the exchange of used smartphones. TEKRIO is a marketplace and verification layer; it is not a party to the transaction between the customer, the retailer and the winning vendor.

2. Eligibility

Retailers and vendors registering on TEKRIO must provide accurate business information, including GST or PAN details where applicable, and are subject to TEKRIO's verification process before receiving full platform access.

3. Pricing & offers

Device offers shown on TEKRIO are provided directly by independent, verified vendors and may vary based on device condition, market demand and vendor assessment at the time of the exchange. TEKRIO does not guarantee any specific price.

4. Retailer & vendor conduct

Retailers and vendors agree to represent device condition, grading and ownership accurately and to conduct exchanges in good faith. TEKRIO reserves the right to suspend accounts that violate these terms.

5. Limitation of liability

TEKRIO and Anabriya Technologies LLP are not liable for disputes arising directly between customers, retailers and vendors, though we encourage all parties to report issues to support@tekrio.in.

6. Changes to these terms

We may update these Terms & Conditions and this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

7. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka having jurisdiction.

8. Contact
