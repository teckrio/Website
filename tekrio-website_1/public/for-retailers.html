Why retailers join TEKRIO

₹

Extra commission

Earn a commission on every device transacted through your store, on top of your existing margins.

◆

More footfall

Customers selling on TEKRIO complete their transaction at your store — bringing more walk-ins and new-device sales with them.

✓

No bargaining

You never need to find buyers or haggle again — verified buyers make their offers on the marketplace.

⏱

Guided listing

A step-by-step inspection in the TEKRIO app records condition, photos and IMEI, then buyer offers come back to you.

Why partner as a retailer

You stay the trusted bridge — TEKRIO does the rest

Your customers already trust you with their pre-owned device. TEKRIO doesn't change that relationship. It gives you offers from verified buyers on the TEKRIO marketplace, instead of the one or two buyers you know personally.

Once registered, you'll get access to the TEKRIO retailer app App access pending to inspect and list devices, track buyer offers and see your commission after every transaction.
