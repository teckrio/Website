A marketplace built so every pre-owned device finds the right buyer

TEKRIO is a pre-owned device marketplace connecting three sides, Customer, Retailer and Buyer, in one transparent ecosystem.

Why we exist

One shop's price was never the market price

Pre-owned devices are usually sold at whatever single price the nearest shop is willing to offer. Customers rarely know what their device is really worth, retailers have no easy way to get a better deal for them, and genuine buyers struggle to find verified, well-described stock.

TEKRIO was built to close that gap: let multiple verified buyers make offers on every device, keep the retailer as the trusted human point of contact, and let the customer see the whole market before they decide.

What TEKRIO is

A marketplace for pre-owned devices

TEKRIO doesn't buy devices and doesn't set prices. It's the neutral marketplace that lets a device be inspected and listed once, so verified buyers can make their offers and the customer can choose.

Customer compares offers and chooses the one that suits them. Buyer makes offers to source genuine, inspected stock. Retailer is the trusted bridge who inspects the device and completes the transaction face-to-face.

What we stand for

The principles behind TEKRIO

Transparency first

Every offer on TEKRIO is visible to the customer before they accept it. No single take-it-or-leave-it price.

Verification, always

Buyers are verified before they can make offers, and devices are recorded with condition details, photos and IMEI before any offer is finalised.

The retailer stays central

TEKRIO strengthens the local retailer's role instead of replacing it — more footfall, more commission, more trust.

The company

Based in Bengaluru

TEKRIO is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We build the technology, verification and trust systems that keep every side of the TEKRIO network — customers, retailers and buyers — fairly served.
