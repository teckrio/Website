Built so every old phone finds its fairest price

TEKRIO is an initiative by Anabriya Technologies LLP — a 3-sided platform connecting customers, retailers and vendors around one honest exchange.

Why we exist

One shop's price was never the market price

Every year, millions of old phones get exchanged across India at whatever single price the nearest shop is willing to offer. Customers rarely know what their device is really worth, retailers have no easy way to get a better deal for them, and genuine vendors struggle to find verified, well-described stock to buy.

TEKRIO was built to close that gap: put verified vendors in real competition for every device, keep the retailer as the trusted human point of contact, and let the customer see the whole market before they decide.

What TEKRIO is

A marketplace, not a middleman

TEKRIO doesn't buy phones and doesn't set prices. It's the neutral layer that lets a retailer list a device once and have a pan-India network of GST-verified vendors bid on it — in minutes, in front of the customer.

Customer gets the best value for their old device. Vendor gives their best price to win genuine stock. Retailer is the trusted bridge that makes the whole exchange happen face-to-face.

What we stand for

The principles behind TEKRIO

Transparency first

Every offer on TEKRIO is visible to the customer before they accept it. No hidden charges, no single take-it-or-leave-it price.

Verification, always

Vendors are GST-verified and devices are recorded with grading, photos and IMEI before any offer is finalised.

The retailer stays central

TEKRIO strengthens the local retailer's role instead of replacing it — more footfall, more commission, more trust.

The company

Anabriya Technologies LLP

TEKRIO is developed and operated by Anabriya Technologies LLP, headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Anabriya builds the technology, verification and trust systems that keep every side of the TEKRIO network — customers, retailers and vendors — fairly served.
