Why we exist

One shop's price was never the market price

Pre-owned devices are usually sold at whatever single price the nearest shop is willing to offer. Customers rarely know what their device is really worth, retailers have no easy way to get a better deal for them, and genuine buyers struggle to find verified, well-described stock.

TEKRIO was built to close that gap: let multiple verified buyers make offers on every device, keep the retailer as the trusted human point of contact, and bring the customer the highest offer the market will pay.

What TEKRIO is

A marketplace for pre-owned devices

TEKRIO doesn't buy devices and doesn't set prices. It's the neutral marketplace that lets a device be inspected and listed once, so verified buyers can make offers and the customer gets the highest one.

Customer gets the highest offer on their device, without haggling. Buyer makes offers to source genuine, inspected stock. Retailer is the trusted bridge who inspects the device and completes the transaction face-to-face.

What we stand for

The principles behind TEKRIO

Transparency first

Verified buyers compete for every device, and the customer sees the offer before accepting it. Any price change needs their approval.

Verification, always

Buyers are verified before they can make offers, and devices are recorded with condition details, photos and IMEI before any offer is finalised.

The retailer stays central

TEKRIO strengthens the local retailer's role instead of replacing it — more footfall, more commission, more trust.

The company

Based in Bengaluru

TEKRIO is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We build the technology, verification and trust systems that keep every side of the TEKRIO network — customers, retailers and buyers — fairly served.
