Frequently asked questions

Answers for customers, retailers and buyers. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

For Customers

Start on our Sell Your Device page: select your device, add its details and share your contact information. A partnered store verifies the device, buyers make offers, and you choose the one you want. You can also walk into any TEKRIO partnered store.
Offers are made by independent, verified buyers based on your device's model and condition. You see every offer before you decide, not just one shop's quote, and you're under no obligation to accept. An offer may change if the partnered store finds the device's actual condition differs from the details you shared.
Payment is completed at the partnered store once you accept an offer and hand over your device. We recommend backing up and resetting your device before the sale.
No. TEKRIO is built to be transparent. If the partnered store finds that your device's condition differs from the details you shared, the offer may change, and the store will explain why before you confirm. You can still decline.

For Retailers

Fill out the registration form on our For Retailers page with your store name, owner details, mobile, city and GSTIN (optional). Our onboarding team will contact you next.
Once onboarded, you'll inspect and list devices through the TEKRIO retailer app — device details, condition and photos — and verified buyers make their offers. App access pending
You earn a commission on every device transacted through TEKRIO at your store. Commission details are shared during onboarding.

For Buyers

Submit your business details on our For Buyers page, including your GST or PAN. Our team verifies buyers before granting access to make offers.
Every listing includes condition details, photos and IMEI, captured by the partnered store at the time of listing.
Yes. Your marketplace feed shows listings from the partnered stores you have access to, and you can make offers on as many as you like.
