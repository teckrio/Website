    Here's what happens next:

    1. Buyers make offers — verified buyers are now making offers on your device.
    2. Get your best offer — we email you the highest offer with a link to accept it. There's no obligation to accept.
    3. Complete transaction — once you accept, your offer page shows a handover code. The buyer collects your device from your address within 60 minutes: give them the code, get paid on the spot, then confirm the payment on your offer page.

    Please note: the price may change if the buyer finds the device's condition differs from what you described. You decide whether to accept a new price.
